description: Review and research output are claims to verify. Legal or factual claims need the primary source inline before anyone acts on them.
type: feedback
---

Secondary sources and model output are for discovery; the primary source is for citation. For law that
means the official gazette: issue number and article, confirmed in force on the day of checking, with
the amendment chain closed. Anything not verified that way is labelled unverified in the file itself.

**Why:** a review of a contract package made confident, specific citations that turned out to be wrong,
and they were caught only after the documents had been built. It cost a rebuild. Separately, a document
quoted a regulation verbatim and correctly from its original gazette issue, four years after it had been
repealed.

**How to apply:** discover broadly, verify narrowly, cite precisely. A critic's findings get the same
verification pass as the original claims. State conclusions only as widely as the verified text supports.
